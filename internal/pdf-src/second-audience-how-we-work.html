// how we work

What this scan is, and how it came about.

You have received a scan of your website without asking for one. That rightly raises a few questions: who we are, where these findings come from, and what happens next. This document answers them before you have to ask. It accompanies every scan we send and it applies in full.

what we retrieved

Public pages, with ordinary requests

We requested a limited number of publicly accessible pages from your website using standard HTTP requests, exactly as your own browser or a search engine does. We also read your robots.txt, your sitemap and the public metadata in the page source. Nothing more, and we honour the instructions in your robots.txt.

what we did not do

No test, no intrusion, no contact

We did not log in, did not access any protected part of your site and did not circumvent any security measure. We did not complete or submit a form and we contacted no one through your site. This is not a penetration test, not a vulnerability assessment and not a load test; we do not look for weaknesses in your systems.

how agent answers are used

Illustration, never the finding

We do put questions to AI agents such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, each in a fresh session, and we save every answer as a dated screenshot. Those answers illustrate what a visitor can be told today. They are never the finding, because an AI answer cannot be reproduced on demand. Every finding in a scan is a piece of text on a page, with the literal sentence and the URL alongside it, and that can be reproduced. We do not predict what an AI system will say about you tomorrow.

personal data

As little as possible, and only what you publish yourself

We do not collect personal data as an objective. Where a name, job title or business email address appears in the scan, it is solely because you published it yourself on your website in a business context, and only where it is needed to support a finding. We do not build profiles of individuals, we do not combine your data with other sources, and we do not sell or share anything with third parties. The basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in being able to make you a business offer. After a free scan we keep only the report and delete the retrieved pages. If you become a client, we keep the retrieved pages for as long as the engagement needs them for re-scans. One email is enough to have everything deleted sooner.

your content stays yours

We take nothing over and we publish nothing

All rights in your website and its content remain entirely yours. Where we quote, these are short fragments serving as evidence for a finding, with the page they appear on named. We do not publish your scan, we do not use it as an example to others, and we name you nowhere as a reference client without your explicit consent. We derive no right whatsoever from this scan over you, your content or your website.

third-party names

No affiliation, no endorsement

ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, Google and other names and services mentioned are the property of their respective owners. We are not affiliated with them, do not act on their instructions and do not speak on their behalf. We name them only to describe what kind of system reads your pages.

// what you may do with it

The report is yours, and there are no strings.

what you may do with the scan

Anything you like, within your own organisation

You may share the scan internally, forward it to your web developer or a third party, and fix the findings yourself, with us or without us. No permission is needed and nothing is expected in return. We ask only that you do not publish it openly, because it contains your own findings.

what this report is not

Not advice, and a snapshot

This is not legal, tax, compliance or investment advice. We are not lawyers. What a finding means for you is for you and your advisers to decide. The scan is also a measurement at a single moment, with the date and time stated: websites change. We do not predict what an AI system will say about you and we guarantee no result, including after work has been carried out.

rulings we cite

Named so that you can read them yourself

Where we mention a court ruling or an enforcement decision, it is stated with its name, date and source. We name these as context for a finding, not as advice about your situation. Some rulings are still open to appeal; where that is the case, we say so.

if a finding is wrong

Tell us and we will take it out

Every finding traces back to a retrieval we can repeat in your presence. If you think something is wrong, let us know: we redo the measurement with you watching and correct or withdraw the finding if you are right. If you have paid for a report and a finding cannot be reproduced, we refund that report.

if you want to hear nothing more

One reply is enough

We contacted you at an address published on your own website. If you would rather not hear from us again, reply to our email with a single line or write to dpo@meihuizen.ai. We will then delete the scan, the retrieved pages and your data, and you do not have to explain why. You also have the right to access, correct or erase your data, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt) or with the data protection authority where you live.

responsible

MB Meihuizen AI, run by Nico Meihuizen

Responsible for this scan and for the processing of the data used in it. Questions about your data go to dpo@meihuizen.ai, everything else to info@meihuizen.ai. We answer within five working days. Full privacy notice: meihuizen.ai/privacy.html.
