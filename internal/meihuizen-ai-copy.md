## Notes on the approach

- **"We" vs "I":** meihuizen.ai LLC is framed as the company voice ("we"), with the About page naming Nico as founder in the third person, then shifting back to "we" for the company's working philosophy. This is a common pattern for solo-founder LLCs; it reads as a company today and scales cleanly if you bring on collaborators later. If you'd rather keep first-person "I" throughout (a more personal, founder-led brand), say so and I'll rewrite it that way instead.
- **sales-intel repo:** I pulled its value proposition (momentum detection on stalling deals, reasoning not just summarizing) into one homepage sentence as a concrete example of "AI-native software", since it's an early-stage MVP and not yet a case study on the live site, I kept the mention light rather than presenting it as a shipped product. Once it has a proper case-study page, that sentence can point directly to it.
- **No invented facts:** I didn't add anything about company size, incorporation details, location, or team; I couldn't find a public LLC record for meihuizen.ai, so I left that out rather than guessing. If you want incorporation/location details included (state, year founded, etc.), send them over and I'll fold them in.
- **Kept:** the Einstein quote and the digital-nomad/quality-of-life line from your current About page; they're distinctive and read as authentic, so I recontextualized rather than cut them.
