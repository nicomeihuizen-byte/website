~/vision

Your next buyer meets you through an agent.

It reads your site, compares you with three others and answers before anyone picks up the phone. It answers whether or not you gave it anything to answer with. Most companies have not noticed yet. The agent does not care.

Two things follow. Companies have to be readable by the machines that now do the first round of buying. And one senior person with agents has the reach a department used to need. We build for both: sites and data that agents get right, and agents that do real work.
