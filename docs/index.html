~/how we like to work

Small steps. A decision after every one.

Not packages to choose from: four steps that build on each other. After each one you decide whether we continue. No long commitment up front, no surprises halfway.

  1. 01you decide
    understand

    Map the problem

    We sit with the people who do the work, write down the constraints, and agree what success looks like. You get a short foundation to build on.

  2. 02you decide
    prototype

    A working version

    Something you can click, test and show around, built on the one journey that matters most. Not slides.

  3. 03you decide
    production

    Built to run

    Tests, a security check, monitoring and a real deployment. AI writes fast; this is where the brakes go on.

  4. 04
    ongoing

    Kept and improved

    New features, maintenance and re-checks, for as long as it earns its place in your business.

~/principles

What we don't compromise on.

  1. 01
    AI + HumanEvery build has two workers: a career in enterprise IT, and a model that reads faster and is wrong more confidently.
  2. 02
    Not on the site is an answerNo estimate, no plausible filler. If it is not written down, we say so.
  3. 03
    Nothing goes out unconfirmedDetails read back, the visitor says yes, then it acts.
  4. 04
    Old claims stay visibleWhen we change our mind, the old line stays with a date. A page that was never wrong was never edited.
  5. 05
    Small on purposeClose enough to understand what you are trying to do. No delivery team in between.
