meihuizen.ai: We Didn't Start With AI. We Started With a Terminal.

I got my first computer at 12: an IBM 5110, no tutorials, just a terminal and a manual. I taught myself HTML and JavaScript there, building websites for fun. That hobby ran alongside a career in enterprise IT sales, pitching data center, cloud, and SaaS at IBM, SoftLayer, Oracle, and Minitab to buyers who could smell a shallow pitch instantly.

Years of pitching taught one habit: understand a product inside out before you present it. Then came the pivot, retraining through Metana's AI Software Engineering program. I now lead meihuizen.ai as an AI-native software engineer, stacking that coding background, sales instinct, and AI training on top of each other. AI isn't a feature here. It's how the work gets built.

The stack: TypeScript, Python, React, LLM tooling, agentic workflows, shipped and monitored, not demoed once and forgotten. No computer science degree, and that's fine. Ask about a decision and get a straight answer, including the ones that didn't work first try.

I build for quality of life and the freedom to work on my own terms, including as a digital nomad. Small on purpose: close enough to actually understand what you're trying to accomplish, and quick to show up in person when it helps. No far-away delivery team in between.
