// overview

AI-driven off-grid farming and homesteading

This project follows two plots across the seasons: one farmed through summer and one through winter, with the hardware, code, training data, and failures documented in public through 2026/2027.

Raspberry Pi soil and weather sensor stations

Raspberry Pi-powered weather and soil stations are housed in a solar-powered birdbox on a stake. Wired ground sensors measure moisture, temperature, and nutrients.

LSTM solar forecasting and battery health prediction

Time-series forecasting models use LSTM techniques to track solar production and battery health.

LLM planting scheduler for biodynamic farming

An LLM-driven planting scheduler combines the biodynamic calendar with global soil and weather datasets.

Compost, fertilizer, and food inventory tracking

Anomaly detection supports compost and fertilizer timing, while a lightweight vision and recommendation model tracks food inventory. The system is designed around clear states and useful feedback as the data grows.
