AI Native Sales-Cycle Control

A focused, single-feature sales intelligence tool that reasons about momentum across a deal's entire note history, flags deals that have gone quiet, and explains why in plain English instead of just summarizing the notes.

// overview

Why this isn't a chatbot wrapper

The easy version of this project is pasting a deal's notes into a prompt and printing whatever comes back. This does something harder: it reasons about momentum. The model weighs how long since the last touch, whether recent notes show forward motion or stalling language, and how that compares to the deal's earlier trajectory, then returns a structured status plus a short reasoning string explaining why.

Three status-aware questions, not one

An open deal gets a momentum read: healthy, stalling, or at risk. A deal already marked lost gets a different question instead, a loss post-mortem that either confirms the loss was final or names a specific unaddressed objection worth revisiting. A won deal gets a win analysis: what pattern made it close, and what's repeatable in a future deal. Three genuinely different prompts and schemas, dispatched from one route based on the deal's own status.

Built for realistic sales cycles

Seed data includes two enterprise-scale deals modeled on multi-quarter B2B cycles, each with 50 or more timestamped notes spanning over a year, champion departures, stalled quarters, and recoveries included, to stress-test the reasoning against something closer to a real Oracle- or IBM-sized deal than a five-note demo.

Status

Actively being built in public, phase by phase: auth, core CRUD, the AI insight feature, and seed data are all live. Every commit ships a working, demoable state rather than partial work.
