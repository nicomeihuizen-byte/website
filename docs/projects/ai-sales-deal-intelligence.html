~/overview

You already own a CRM.

Be honest about the last time anyone filled it in.

Five is not that. Five slots to keep the focus, and one line written after each call. It reads the history and tells you which deal has stopped moving and why.

That is what a good sales manager does in a pipeline review, and what most teams this size have never had.

Nico runs his own pipeline in it, daily. The one-line note was designed by someone who hated filling in CRMs for fourteen years.
