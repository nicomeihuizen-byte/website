Agent Fritz

The chat in the corner of this page is the product. Ask it anything about meihuizen.ai: it reads the pages it needs, answers from what they say, shows you which ones, and says so when something is not on the site. Named after Nico's dog, who also only brings back what is actually there.

On this site Fritz is configured as a sales assistant: it qualifies leads and takes on real pre-sales and inside sales work. That is our version. Fritz can be whatever your business needs, a customer care rep, a doctor's assistant, a support desk: buy it as it is, or have it built around your regulatory requirements.

// overview

Why most website chatbots are a liability

A chatbot that answers from a model's general knowledge fills gaps with something plausible. Under your logo, a plausible wrong price or an invented company detail is your mistake, not the model's. This site keeps a dated correction for exactly that kind of claim on its credentials page: a legal entity stated as fact before it existed.

What Fritz does

  • Answers from your pages, in the visitor's language and in the formal register your site uses, with the source under every answer.
  • Hands over documents. Ask for a product sheet and it is there as a download. Ask for one that does not exist and it says so, and offers to have someone send it.
  • Books the meeting. It offers real free times from the calendar, reads the details back and sends the invite with a video link. Intro calls, demos and longer meetings.
  • Passes leads on. The visitor's question, contact details and the whole conversation land in the inbox, with one line on what the visitor said about their situation: the pain, who decides, the timeline.
  • Knows the time. Outside office hours it says so, names the moment someone is back, and before a weekend asks whether it can wait.
  • Brings a human in. When Nico is available, Fritz asks the visitor whether they want him in the chat and rings his phone. Nico joins the same conversation.
  • Takes instructions in that conversation. With Nico in the chat, he can hand Fritz a task from his phone ("plan a 30-minute demo next week", "share the product sheet"). Fritz does it and gives the conversation back. Only Nico can; a visitor typing the same words gets nowhere.
  • Feeds Five, as an option. Leads Fritz qualifies can land in Five as FQLs, Fritz Qualified Leads: in an incoming queue, not in your five slots. Fritz qualifies; you decide what gets a slot.

How it sells

Fritz works the way a good inside sales rep works, with three methods. SPIN to ask the one question that shows a visitor what a problem costs them. Challenger to correct a belief with a fact from the site rather than an argument. MEDDPICC to notice who decides and when, so the lead that reaches you is already qualified. The methods decide what it asks. They never decide what it claims: every fact still comes from a page.

Guardrails that live on the server

A prompt is a request. These are rules the server enforces, whatever the model says:

  • No email address, phone number or domain is used unless the visitor typed it, and nothing is sent until it has been read back and confirmed.
  • An address on a domain that does not exist or takes no mail goes back to the visitor to check, before it reaches you as a lead that bounces.
  • A phone number that is not on the site never reaches the screen.
  • Only the site owner can give Fritz instructions, and only from his own phone.

Tested with traps

A fixed set of questions runs against Fritz before a change ships, and the traps are the point: the VAT number that has not been issued yet, the LLC that never existed, the team of engineers that is one person, the case company it must not name. A wrong answer on a trap means the change does not go out. Test runs send nothing, book nothing and ring no one.

What you get on your site

The same build, pointed at your pages. The knowledge is rebuilt from your content, so editing a page and changing what the agent knows are one change. Your languages, answering in the visitor's. The actions wired to your inbox, your calendar and your systems. A test set written for the traps in your business. A spend cap, per-visitor limits, and a switch that turns it off without a deploy. The function runs in Frankfurt and conversations are not logged: the server keeps counts, not content, except the conversation a visitor sends as a request.

What it costs

Fritz is a custom build, not a wrapper around a chatbot. Getting it right for a business takes time: your content, your languages, your actions, your traps, tested before anything ships. The price follows that work: how many pages, how many languages, which actions it takes. Starting from a Second Audience scan, it is priced on the value the scan shows. Without one, tell Nico what your site covers and he will work out a price with you. We ship AI plus a human: Nico builds it, tests it and stays on it.

Status

Live on this site since September 2026, answering every visitor in their own language. No client deployment yet: this site is the first, and the one you can test hardest.
