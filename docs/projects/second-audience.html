~/method

Nine steps. The agents come last.

The order is the product. A scan that starts at the agents finds technical imperfections and no money. Every strong finding this year came out of step four.

dual-render diffingrobots.txt & UA verificationverdict taxonomymulti-agent evaluationserver-log forensicsregulatory cross-check
  1. 01

    Ask what buyers are told to check

    Before we name you, we ask an agent what a buyer in your market should check. The test questions come from the market, not from us.

  2. 02

    Find the revenue object

    The page that makes the money. Everything after this is measured against it.

  3. 03

    Extract the facts a buyer needs

    Price, terms, who it is for, where you are. The answers an agent will be asked for.

  4. 04

    Hunt for the same fact, answered twice

    where the money findings come from

    An FAQ, a stale translation, an orphan page, a press release from four years ago. Where agents have to choose.

  5. 05

    Check how each fact is written

    Text, image, or hedged prose. Only one of the three survives a machine read.

  6. 06

    Walk your funnel on the live site

    A person clicks your ad and follows the path your buyer takes, recording what is live at each step. Agents often answer from an older copy; the difference is a finding.

  7. 07

    Map what third parties say

    Directories, guides and press that agents trust as much as your own site.

  8. 08

    Regulatory cross-check

    First to kill findings that turn out to be wording the law requires. Then to strengthen the ones that survive.

  9. 09

    Predictions first, then the agents

    What we expect each agent to say, written down before asking. Then the questions buyers really type, typos included.

~/what a finding looks like

An agent that finds nothing rarely says nothing.

It fills the gap, and puts your name under what it filled in. Two real scans, anonymised: no names, no searchable figures, and we do not confirm guesses.

on the site: One client case page: a template never filled in, still in its own sitemap.

the agents: Four agents, four different answers. One invented a full case at a real bank and named the company as the source three times.

the fix: Real cases with a number, one line of text beside each logo, the template out of the sitemap. No rebuild.

case 05 · online education provider

on the site: The same programme live at three addresses, with different prices, durations and guarantees.

the agents: One agent sent the buyer to the oldest page, with the shorter guarantee. Its citation was correct: that text really is there.

the fix: One live offer, the old addresses pointed at it, and the sitemap cleaned.

~/who does what

A person and a model, working the same case.

Both are wrong regularly. Each has to answer to the other before anything ships.

the machine does the volume

  • Every page fetched twice and compared.
  • Every business fact diffed against every other instance of itself on the domain.
  • Every cited document opened, to check its text holds what the citation claims.

the person does what a model can't

  • Decides which object carries the revenue, which fact is worth chasing, and whether a citation actually holds.
  • Walks your funnel as a buyer, and asks the agents the way a buyer types.
  • For the full report: asks your sales lead what lost deals hinged on, tests your sales process as a buyer, and signs the verdict.

~/questions buyers ask

Before you ask Fritz.

Or ask him anyway. He answers from this page, and says so when it isn't here.

Is the free scan really free?

Yes. You get a summary of the full scan: what agents told a buyer about you, with timestamps, and what it costs you. Nothing is owed if you stop there.

Why doesn't the free scan show how to fix it?

Because the fix is the work. The free scan proves the problem. The full report shows where each issue sits and what the correct version is, and its price is credited in full if we implement.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. The scan reads your public website the way an agent does, from the outside.

Can we not just add structured data?

Structured data helps, but many agents never see it: they convert your page to text first. Every fact has to stand in the visible text as well.

Will our results be published?

Paying clients never, not even anonymised. Free scans may become anonymised cases: no names, no searchable figures, and we do not confirm guesses.

Which languages?

Reports ship in English. Quoted evidence stays in the language it was captured in.

Why does it repeat?

Engines update and pages change, and a claim you removed keeps being answered for months. There is a dated measurement of exactly that on this domain.
