Second Audience

Your website has two kinds of visitor now. One has eyes. The other is agentic AI: it never runs your JavaScript, reads only what your server sends, and then answers questions about you for its visitors, who never open your page. Second Audience measures what that second visitor can actually do.

A service, not a tool. The scan is run by a person and a model working the same case, because the claim being sold is that machines read confidently and get facts wrong, and that is not a claim a machine can certify about itself.

// overview

Seven steps, in this order, and the agents come last

The order is the product. A scan that starts at the agents finds technical imperfections and no money. Every strong finding this year came out of step three: does the same fact appear somewhere else on the domain, worded differently. An FAQ, a stale translation, an orphan page, a press release from four years ago.

What a finding looks like

A museum's best room costs €299 on weekdays and €325 at the weekend. Its own page says only "from €299". Nine answers from four agents gave €235, €264.50, €277.50, €299 and "between €150 and €350". Not one gave the weekend rate.

A private bank's page title says since 1804. Its own timeline says 1881. The 1804 belongs to the family's textile business, not to the bank. A machine that carries the title away makes the bank seventy-seven years older than it is.

We publish our own misses

Eighteen predictions written down before a single agent was asked. Nine of them wrong. That is in the report, with the reason. A report that shows only the hits is a sales document and gets read as one.

A person and a model, working the same case

The machine does the volume: every page fetched twice and compared, every business fact diffed against every other instance of itself on the domain, every cited document opened to see whether its text layer holds what the citation claims. A person makes the three decisions no loop produces: which object carries the revenue, which fact is worth chasing, and whether a citation actually holds. Both are wrong regularly. Each has to answer to the other before anything ships.

It does not stay fixed

Engines update, pages change, and a claim you have removed keeps being answered for months afterwards. There is a dated measurement of exactly that on this domain. So the work repeats: the same prompts at one week, one month and three months, against the same pages.

Status

Seven scans completed. Two of them are now paying clients who arrived as free scans. Reports ship in Dutch and English, with a two-page annex on scope, data, rights and GDPR attached to every one.
